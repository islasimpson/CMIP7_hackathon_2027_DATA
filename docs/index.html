About CMIP7 variable names

CMIP7 identifies variables by a branded name, <variable>_<branding>. The branding has four parts: temporal (tavg mean, tmaxavg monthly mean of daily max, tpt instantaneous…), vertical (h2m 2 m height, u unspecified, p19 19 pressure levels…), horizontal (hxy lat-lon field, hy zonal mean…) and area (u all, sea, lnd…). For example, tas_tavg-h2m-hxy-u is the CMIP6 Amon/tas equivalent. The Amon-style folders come from realm + frequency.
